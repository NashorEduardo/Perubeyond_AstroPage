## 2. In-Depth Guide to the 15 Best Places to Visit in Peru

### 1. Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu (Cusco)
- **Elevation:** 2,430 meters (7,972 feet).
- **Why visit:** A New 7 Wonder of the World and UNESCO World Heritage site, this masterwork of Incan masonry was constructed under Emperor Pachacútec in the 15th century. Perched on an emerald ridge between Huayna Picchu and Machu Picchu mountain, it overlooks the roaring Urubamba River.
- **Top highlights:** The Sacred Plaza, Intihuatana solar observatory, Temple of the Three Windows, Sacred Rock, and the Temple of the Sun.
- **Peru Beyond Tip:** Book the **Circuit 2 Classic / Panoramic permit** at least 4 months ahead to secure the iconic postcard view from the upper terrace.

### 2. Cusco Historic Center & Qoricancha Sun Temple
- **Elevation:** 3,399 meters (11,152 feet).
- **Why visit:** The ancient capital of the Incan Empire. Here, polished Incan stone foundations support elegant Spanish colonial churches and red-tile balconies.
- **Top highlights:** The Plaza de Armas, the 12-Angled Stone along Calle Hatun Rumiyoc, the artisan neighborhood of San Blas, and Qoricancha (the Sun Temple whose curved walls were once sheeted in pure gold).
- **Featured Tour:** [Cusco City Tour + Qoricancha & 4 Ruins](/en/tours/cusco/city-tour-cusco-qoricancha-4-ruinas).

### 3. Sacred Valley of the Incas: Pisac & Ollantaytambo

- **Elevation:** 2,792 m to 2,972 m (9,160 to 9,750 feet).
- **Why visit:** The agricultural and spiritual heartland along the Vilcanota River. Pisac dazzles with sweeping agricultural terraces clinging to steep cliffs, while Ollantaytambo is the only surviving "Living Inca Town," with stone residences and freshwater channels unchanged since 1450.
- **Featured Tour:** [Sacred Valley Full Day Tour with Buffet Lunch](/en/tours/cusco/valle-sagrado-full-day-almuerzo-buffet).

### 4. Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca)

- **Elevation:** 5,200 meters (17,060 feet).
- **Why visit:** Discovered by international travelers after glacial melting revealed millions of years of weathered sandstone and trace minerals, Vinicunca displays vibrant parallel stripes of lavender, turquoise, gold, and terracotta against the snowy backdrop of Mount Ausangate.
- **Featured Tour:** [Rainbow Mountain Trekking Full Day](/en/tours/cusco/trekking-montana-7-colores).

### 5. Humantay Lake

- **Elevation:** 4,200 meters (13,779 feet).
- **Why visit:** A radiant turquoise alpine jewel fed by the hanging glaciers of Mount Humantay (5,473 m), nestled along the Salkantay trail. Andean travelers leave stone cairns (*apachetas*) here in gratitude to Pachamama (Mother Earth).
- **Featured Tour:** [Humantay Lake Trekking Full Day](/en/tours/cusco/trekking-laguna-humantay-full-day).

### 6. Maras Salt Mines & Moray Agricultural Laboratory

- **Elevation:** 3,380 m to 3,500 m.
- **Why visit:** In Moray, concentric circular terraces create a temperature variance of up to 15°C (27°F) between top and bottom, functioning as an Incan crop acclimatization laboratory. Minutes away, the Maras Salt Pans cascade down a canyon with over 3,000 pink salt pools fed by a subterranean hyper-saline spring.
- **Featured Tour:** [Maras, Moray & Salt Mines Tour](/en/tours/cusco/maras-moray-salineras-tour).

### 7. Chinchero: The Rainbow Weaving Town
- **Elevation:** 3,762 meters (12,342 feet).
- **Why visit:** Perched on a high plateau, Chinchero features the royal palace of Inca Túpac Yupanqui, an ornate colonial church, and women's weaving collectives demonstrating how alpaca wool is hand-spun and dyed with cochineal and native plants.

### 8. Huacachina Desert Oasis (Ica)
- **Elevation:** 400 meters (1,312 feet).
- **Why visit:** South America's only true desert oasis, featuring a natural palm-fringed lagoon surrounded by the continent's tallest dunes. The late-afternoon 4x4 dune buggy rides and sandboarding offer an exhilarating rush and golden desert sunsets.

### 9. Paracas National Reserve & Ballestas Islands
- **Elevation:** 0 meters (Sea Level).
- **Why visit:** Known as the "Peruvian Galápagos," the Ballestas Islands shelter thousands of sea lions, Humboldt penguins, Peruvian boobies, and pelicans. On the boat ride, you'll pass the mysterious 180-meter-tall *Candelabro* geoglyph etched into the coastal cliffs.
- **Featured Tour:** [Lima, Paracas, Huacachina & Machu Picchu 7 Days](/en/tours/peru/tour-lima-paracas-huacachina-machu-picchu-cusco-7-dias).

### 10. Lima: Culinary Capital, Miraflores & Barranco
- **Elevation:** 0 to 150 meters.
- **Why visit:** South America's only coastal capital and home to the world's highest-ranking restaurants (such as Central and Maido). Stroll along Miraflores' clifftop boardwalk, admire Spanish colonial balconies in the Historic Center, and sip pisco sours in artsy Barranco.

### 11. Palcoyo Rainbow Mountain Range
- **Elevation:** 4,900 meters (16,076 feet).
- **Why visit:** The gentle, uncrowded alternative to Vinicunca. Palcoyo offers panoramic views of three distinct colored mountains and an otherworldly geological stone forest via an easy 30-minute walk with virtually no steep climbing.
- **Featured Tour:** [Palcoyo Rainbow Mountain Trek](/en/tours/cusco/trekking-palcoyo).

### 12. 7 Lakes of Ausangate & Pacchanta Hot Springs
- **Elevation:** 4,600 meters (15,091 feet).
- **Why visit:** An awe-inspiring alpine loop connecting seven glacial lakes of turquoise, green, and deep sapphire (such as Azulcocha and Otorongococha) directly beneath the sacred Apu Ausangate (6,384 m), concluding with a soak in natural volcanic hot springs.
- **Featured Tour:** [7 Lakes of Ausangate Trek](/en/tours/cusco/trekking-7-lagunas-ausangate).

### 13. South Valley of Cusco: Tipón, Pikillacta & Andahuaylillas
- **Elevation:** 3,150 m to 3,560 m.
- **Why visit:** A journey spanning three historical eras: Tipón's monumental Incan water temple where aqueducts still channel mountain springs; the sprawling pre-Inca Wari city of Pikillacta; and Andahuaylillas' colonial church, celebrated as the "Sistine Chapel of the Americas" for its gold-leaf altars and painted ceilings.
- **Featured Tour:** [South Valley Cusco Tour](/en/tours/cusco/valle-sur-cusco-tour).

### 14. Nazca Lines and Geoglyphs
- **Elevation:** 520 meters.
- **Why visit:** Massive geometric figures and animal outlines (the hummingbird, monkey, spider, condor) etched into the desert plains between 500 BCE and 500 CE, admired in all their scale via scenic sightseeing flights.

### 15. Lake Titicaca & Colca Canyon
- **Elevation:** 3,812 m (Titicaca) & 3,635 m (Colca).
- **Why visit:** Lake Titicaca is the highest navigable lake in the world, famed for the floating totora-reed islands of the Uros people. Nearby in Arequipa, the dramatic Colca Canyon (twice as deep as the Grand Canyon) offers close-up views of giant Andean condors soaring on morning thermals.

## 3. How to Structure Your Peru Itinerary Logistically

To minimize transit fatigue, group attractions logically:
1. **Coastal & Desert Route (3-4 Days):** Lima cuisine ➔ Paracas (Ballestas Islands) ➔ Ica (Huacachina dunes) ➔ Nazca.
2. **Incan Heritage Route (4-5 Days):** Cusco ➔ Sacred Valley (Pisac, Ollantaytambo, Maras, Moray) ➔ Machu Picchu via scenic train.
3. **High Alpine Trekking (2-3 Days):** Humantay Lake ➔ Vinicunca or Palcoyo ➔ Ausangate 7 Lakes.
4. **Southern Altiplano Extension (3-4 Days):** Scenic bus across the high plains ➔ Lake Titicaca (Puno) ➔ Arequipa & Colca Canyon.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the most visited tourist attraction in Peru?
Machu Picchu is the most visited landmark in Peru, drawing over 1.5 million international travelers each year.

### Which places can I visit on a 7-day trip to Peru?
In one week, you can easily experience Lima's culinary scene, the Sacred Valley, Machu Picchu, and Cusco's Historic Center.

### What are the best things to do in Cusco besides Machu Picchu?
Sacsayhuamán, Qoricancha, Ollantaytambo, Maras Salt Mines, Moray, Humantay Lake, and Rainbow Mountain.

### Which top destinations in Peru have no high altitude?
Lima, Paracas National Reserve, the Ballestas Islands, Huacachina Oasis, and the Nazca Lines all sit near sea level.

### Which is better: Vinicunca Rainbow Mountain or Palcoyo?
Vinicunca is the iconic classic route for active hikers, while Palcoyo offers an easy, short, flat trail ideal for families and casual walkers.

### Is it worth combining Lima, Paracas, and Huacachina before heading to Cusco?
Yes, it is the best acclimatization strategy: it allows you to enjoy desert wildlife and coastal dining at sea level before ascending into the high Andes.
