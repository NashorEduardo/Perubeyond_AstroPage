The scenic **train to Machu Picchu** is celebrated across the travel world as one of the most breathtaking railway journeys on Earth. As the train tracks snake parallel to the sacred Vilcanota (Urubamba) River, the scenery transitions from the terraced agricultural hillsides of the Sacred Valley into the lush, emerald cloud forest guarding the Inca citadel.

Because no direct roads lead to Machu Picchu, the railway serves as the vital, comfortable lifeline for over 90% of international travelers. Yet, choosing the right train can prove puzzling: What is the difference between **PeruRail** and **Inca Rail**? Is the upgrade to **Vistadome** or **The 360°** worth the price? What train offers an open-air observation deck? How do station logistics and luggage limits work?

In this definitive 2026-2027 guide prepared by the operations team and licensed guides at **Peru Beyond**, we break down every service tier, onboard amenities, station routes, and insider strategies to help you select the ideal train for your budget and travel style.

## 2. Who Operates the Train to Machu Picchu?

Two accredited rail companies operate services to Aguas Calientes station:

### PeruRail
Founded in 1999 in partnership with luxury hospitality group **Belmond**, PeruRail is the largest operator with more than 25 daily frequencies during peak season. They operate out of San Pedro (central Cusco), Poroy, and Ollantaytambo stations, spanning the spectrum from standard Expedition cars to the legendary Belmond Hiram Bingham.

### Inca Rail
Established in 2007 by Peruvian hospitality professionals, Inca Rail modernized the rail corridor with elegant carriage aesthetics, warm onboard service, and contemporary technology. Operating primarily from Ollantaytambo and via bimodal coach transfers from Cusco, its crown jewels are The 360° and The First Class cars.

### 3.1. Standard Tourist Class: Expedition vs. The Voyager
These budget-friendly options deliver high comfort, spotless interiors, and panoramic vistas at accessible price points.

#### PeruRail Expedition
- **Features:** Incan-inspired upholstery, spacious four-seat pods with wooden tables, efficient climate control, and overhead luggage racks.
- **Windows:** Large lateral viewing windows supplemented by overhead skylights.
- **Pros:** Abundant departure times throughout the day; reliable seating; affordable rates.
- **Cons:** Food and beverages are available for purchase from the trolley rather than included.

#### Inca Rail The Voyager
- **Features:** Ergonomic fabric seats with fold-down trays, broad viewing windows, and soft instrumental music.
- **Pros:** Quiet, clean ambiance; option to pre-order gourmet boxed lunches.
- **Cons:** Fewer daily departures than PeruRail.

---

### 3.2. Superior Panoramic Class: Vistadome vs. The 360°
This is the most popular choice for travelers seeking immersive canyon photography and cultural entertainment.

#### PeruRail Vistadome & Vistadome Observatory
- **Classic Vistadome:** Wraparound curved windows curve up into the ceiling, creating the feeling of traveling inside a glass bubble through the canyon. On the return trip from Aguas Calientes to Ollantaytambo, passengers enjoy an Andean snack followed by **two live cultural performances**: the energetic Saqra masked folk dance moving through the aisles and a live alpaca wool fashion show.
- **Vistadome Observatory:** Adds an **outdoor viewing balcony** at the rear of the train where you can step out into the mountain breeze to photograph rushing waterfalls and towering granite peaks without glass reflection, alongside a dedicated bar car with live music.
- **Best for:** Couples, families, and photographers who want a lively cultural experience.

#### Inca Rail The 360°
- **Features:** Extra-tall windows, leather seating, and an open-air central observation lounge.
- **Interactive Tech:** Connect to the train's internal Wi-Fi network on your smartphone to listen to location-based audio guides narrating Incan legends and geological landmarks.
- **Pros:** Modern tech amenities, organic snack included, and excellent lighting.

### 3.3. Luxury Class: The First Class vs. Belmond Hiram Bingham
For travelers celebrating milestones or seeking world-class luxury.

#### Inca Rail The First Class
- **The Experience:** Welcomed with a signature Pisco Sour cocktail, live acoustic music, an open-air observation terrace, and a refined 3-course Andean gourmet meal paired with boutique South American wines.
- **Inclusions:** Includes private connecting bus transport up to the citadel gates.

#### Belmond Hiram Bingham
- **The Ultimate Luxury Experience:** Modeled after 1920s Pullman carriages with polished brass, dark mahogany wood, and rich velvet fabrics.
- **What is included in the fare:**
  - 4-course gourmet brunch (outbound) and 4-course dinner (return) curated by renowned chefs.
  - Unlimited open bar with premium spirits, wines, and craft cocktails.
  - Open-air observation car with live traditional Peruvian and international acoustic band.
  - Exclusive VIP Consettur bus service to the citadel.
  - Official Machu Picchu entry ticket with private licensed guide.
  - Traditional afternoon tea at the Belmond Sanctuary Lodge at the citadel entrance.

Understanding where your train leaves from is vital for avoiding missed departures:

### 4.1. Ollantaytambo Station (Sacred Valley)
- **Location:** Approximately 1 hour and 45 minutes by road from Cusco.
- **Why it is the most popular:** It operates 365 days a year without weather disruptions, hosting over 30 combined daily departures. The train ride to Machu Picchu takes only **1 hour and 40 minutes**.

### 4.2. San Pedro & Poroy Stations (Cusco)
- **San Pedro:** Located in downtown Cusco opposite San Pedro Market.
- **Poroy:** Located 25 minutes by taxi outside Cusco.
- **Duration:** 3 hours and 15 minutes to 3 hours and 45 minutes.
- **Seasonal Note:** Direct train services operate from May through December. During the rainy season (January to April), these stations close for safety, transitioning to the Bimodal service.

### 4.3. The Bimodal Service (Bus + Train)
Operated by both PeruRail and Inca Rail: a comfortable private tourist coach takes you from Cusco to Ollantaytambo station, where you seamlessly board your connecting train to Aguas Calientes. Both legs are covered under a single unified ticket.

- **Managing Large Luggage:** Large rolling suitcases (23 kg) are stored safely free of charge in your hotel's locked luggage room in Cusco or Ollantaytambo. You simply bring clothing and toiletries needed for your stay in Aguas Calientes.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the difference between PeruRail Expedition and Vistadome?
Expedition is the comfortable standard class, while Vistadome provides larger overhead glass domes, a complimentary snack, and live folk dance and alpaca fashion performances on the return trip.

### Which train company is better: PeruRail or Inca Rail?
Both companies share the same tracks and maintain superb standards. PeruRail offers more daily departures and the ultra-luxury Hiram Bingham, while Inca Rail features The 360° and The First Class.

### How long does the train ride from Ollantaytambo take?
The journey takes between 1 hour and 35 minutes and 1 hour and 45 minutes.

### Which side of the train has the best river views?
On the way to Machu Picchu, sit on the left side of the carriage. On the return journey back to Ollantaytambo, sit on the right side.

### Can I bring my large wheeled suitcase on the train?
No, trains strictly enforce a hand luggage limit of 5 to 8 kg. Heavy suitcases are stored safely for free at your hotel in Cusco or Ollantaytambo.

### How far in advance should I buy train tickets?
Purchase tickets 2 to 4 months ahead for regular season, and 4 to 6 months ahead for peak dry season (May through October).

### Do Peru Beyond packages include round-trip train tickets?
Yes, all our Machu Picchu tours include guaranteed round-trip train tickets in your preferred class, private station transfers, bus passes, and expert guided visits.
